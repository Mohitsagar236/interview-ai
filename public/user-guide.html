Getting Started

Welcome to Interview AI! This guide will help you set up and start using your AI-powered interview assistant in minutes.

What is Interview AI?

Interview AI is a desktop application that listens to your interview questions in real-time and provides AI-generated answers instantly. It uses advanced speech recognition and ChatGPT to help you respond confidently during job interviews.

Real-time Listening

Automatically captures interview questions as they're asked

AI-Powered Answers

Generates intelligent responses using ChatGPT

100% Private

Your data never leaves your computer

System Requirements

  • Operating System: Windows 10+, macOS 11.0+, or Linux (Ubuntu 18.04+)
  • RAM: 4 GB minimum (8 GB recommended)
  • Disk Space: 500 MB free space
  • Internet: Active connection required
  • Audio: Working microphone or system audio

Installation Guide

1

Download the App

Visit the download page and click the download button for your platform (Windows, macOS, or Linux).

2

Run the Installer

Locate the downloaded file and double-click to run the installer. On Windows, click "Yes" when prompted by User Account Control.

3

Follow Setup Wizard

The installation wizard will guide you through the process. Choose your installation directory and click "Install".

4

Launch the App

Once installation is complete, launch Interview AI from your desktop or start menu.

5

Create an Account

Sign up for a free account or log in if you already have one. You'll need this to sync your credits and settings.

Pro Tip:

Grant microphone permissions when prompted. This is essential for Interview AI to hear and transcribe interview questions.

Your First Interview Session

Starting a Session

1

Click "Start Session"

Open Interview AI and click the "Start Session" button on the main dashboard.

2

Configure Settings

Choose your interview type (Technical, Behavioral, or General) and adjust audio settings if needed.

3

Begin Interview

Start your video call interview. Interview AI will automatically listen and transcribe questions in real-time.

4

Review AI Suggestions

As questions are detected, AI-generated answers will appear on your screen. Use them as guidance for your responses.

5

End Session

When the interview is complete, click "End Session" to save your session history and review performance.

Important:

Make sure Interview AI is running BEFORE you join your interview call. Position the window where you can see it without being obvious on camera.

Key Features

Real-time Audio Transcription

Interview AI uses advanced speech recognition to transcribe interview questions as they're spoken. This works with any video conferencing platform (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, etc.).

AI-Generated Answers

Powered by ChatGPT, Interview AI generates intelligent, contextual answers to interview questions. The AI understands technical questions, behavioral scenarios, and coding challenges.

Screen Capture & OCR

If an interviewer shares their screen with a coding question or technical problem, Interview AI can capture and read the text using OCR technology.

Resume Context

Upload your resume to provide context. Interview AI will tailor answers based on your experience and skills.

Multiple Interview Types

  • Technical Interviews: System design, architecture questions, and technical concepts
  • Behavioral Interviews: STAR method responses for situational questions
  • Coding Interviews: Algorithm explanations and code optimization tips

Session History

Review past sessions, questions asked, and answers provided. Learn from each interview to improve your performance.

Tips & Tricks

Best Practices

  • Test Your Setup: Run a practice session before your real interview to ensure audio is working properly
  • Microphone Placement: Position your microphone to pick up both your voice and the interviewer's (system audio works best)
  • Dual Monitor Setup: Use a second monitor to view Interview AI suggestions while maintaining eye contact on camera
  • Natural Responses: Don't read answers verbatim. Use AI suggestions as talking points and respond in your own words
  • Practice First: Use Interview AI for mock interviews to get comfortable with the interface

Maximizing AI Quality

  • Clear Audio: Ensure good audio quality for accurate transcription
  • Upload Resume: Provide your resume for personalized, context-aware answers
  • Specify Role: Tell the AI what position you're interviewing for
  • Note Taking: Jot down key points from AI suggestions before responding
Pro Tip:

Use Interview AI as a safety net, not a crutch. The goal is to boost your confidence and fill knowledge gaps, not replace genuine conversation. Interviewers can tell when you're reading from a script!

Troubleshooting

Audio Not Detected

  • Check microphone permissions in your system settings
  • Ensure Interview AI has permission to access audio
  • Try selecting a different audio input device in settings
  • Restart the application and try again

Poor Transcription Quality

  • Reduce background noise
  • Move closer to the microphone
  • Check internet connection (required for transcription service)
  • Adjust audio input volume in system settings

AI Not Generating Answers

  • Verify you have credits remaining (check your profile)
  • Ensure stable internet connection
  • Check that the question was transcribed correctly
  • Try rephrasing or waiting for the complete question

App Crashes or Freezes

  • Close other resource-intensive applications
  • Check for app updates and install if available
  • Restart your computer
  • Reinstall the application if problems persist

Credits or Activation Issues

  • Verify your account is logged in
  • Check activation code is entered correctly
  • Ensure device is connected to the internet
  • Contact support if activation fails repeatedly
