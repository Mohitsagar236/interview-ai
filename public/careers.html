Hiring process

A clear process with practical signal.

We focus on your work, your thinking, and how you approach real product problems.

  1. 1

    Submit Application

    Tell us what you have built and which role fits you best.

    Same day confirmation
  2. 2

    Portfolio Review

    We review your GitHub, portfolio, resume, and written answers.

    2-4 days
  3. 3

    Technical Assignment

    A scoped assignment designed around realistic AI or product engineering work.

    3-5 days
  4. 4

    Interview

    Discuss your approach, tradeoffs, ownership style, and learning mindset.

    45-60 minutes
  5. 5

    Within one week

FAQs

Common questions before you apply.

Is this remote?

Yes. Interview AI is remote first and works with async communication.

Can students apply?

Yes. Students can apply if they can commit to the role expectations and show strong building ability.

What is the stipend?

The current stipend for listed full-time internship roles is ₹40,000 per month.

Do I need prior AI experience?

Prior AI experience helps, but strong fundamentals, curiosity, and shipped projects matter more.

Will I receive feedback?

We aim to provide feedback after practical assignments and interviews whenever possible.

How long does the hiring process take?

Most candidates move through the process in one to three weeks, depending on assignment timing.

Can freshers apply?

Yes. Freshers can apply with strong projects, clear communication, and a fast learning mindset.

Is there a PPO opportunity?

Yes. Exceptional performers may be considered for a pre-placement offer based on impact and team fit.
