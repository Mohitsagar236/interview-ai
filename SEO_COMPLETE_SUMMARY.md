## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ **Don't buy backlinks** - Google will penalize you
2. ❌ **Don't keyword stuff** - Use keywords naturally
3. ❌ **Don't copy content** - All content must be original
4. ❌ **Don't ignore mobile** - 60% of searches are mobile
5. ❌ **Don't expect instant results** - SEO takes 3-6 months minimum
6. ❌ **Don't neglect page speed** - Slow sites rank lower
7. ❌ **Don't forget about user experience** - It matters!
8. ❌ **Don't ignore Search Console warnings** - Fix issues promptly
