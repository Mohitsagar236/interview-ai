Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Interview AI

How does Interview AI work?

Interview AI is a desktop application that listens to your interview questions in real-time and provides AI-generated answers instantly. It uses advanced speech recognition and ChatGPT to help you respond confidently.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes! All processing happens locally on your computer. Your interview data is never stored on our servers. We take your privacy seriously.

What types of interviews does it support?

Interview AI works with all types of interviews including behavioral, technical, coding, and system design interviews. It's especially powerful for coding interview questions.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes, an active internet connection is required for AI responses, as the app sends queries to your chosen AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, etc.). All other processing — transcription, OCR, and resume search — runs locally.

Can I use it for multiple interviews?

Absolutely! The app is 100% free with no session limits. You pay only for the AI tokens you consume directly to your API provider — Interview AI itself never charges you.

Which AI providers are supported?

OpenAI (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini), Anthropic (Claude), Groq (Llama, Mixtral), Google Gemini, xAI (Grok), and OpenRouter — which gives you access to 200+ models with one key.
