## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Don't:**
- Keyword stuff your content
- Buy backlinks
- Copy content from other sites
- Hide text or links
- Use black hat SEO tactics
- Give up after 1 month
- Ignore mobile optimization
- Neglect user experience

✅ **Do:**
- Write for humans, optimize for search engines
- Build quality backlinks naturally
- Create original, helpful content
- Be transparent and ethical
- Use white hat SEO only
- Be patient (3-6 months minimum)
- Prioritize mobile experience
- Focus on user value
